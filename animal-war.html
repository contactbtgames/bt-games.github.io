About Animal War: Conquer the State

Animal War: Conquer the State is a strategy conquest game developed by BT-Games. Lead your animal army into battle and take over the state.

Plan your attacks, grow your forces and expand your territory in fun, fast-paced battles!

Key Game Features

🦁 Animal Army

Command a squad of fierce animals.

🗺️ Conquer the State

Capture territory step by step.

⚔️ Strategic Battles

Outsmart your opponents.

🎮 Easy to Play

Simple controls for everyone.

How to Play

  • Build up your animal army.
  • Attack neighboring territory to expand.
  • Conquer the entire state to win.
