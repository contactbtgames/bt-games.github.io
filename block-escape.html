About Block Escape

Block Escape is a brain-teasing block puzzle game developed by BT-Games. Slide and rearrange blocks to clear a path and escape.

With many handcrafted levels that grow steadily more challenging, Block Escape is perfect for quick breaks and long puzzle sessions alike!

Easy one-touch play.

Play anytime, anywhere.

How to Play

  • Move the blocks to clear a path to the exit.
  • Plan your moves carefully to solve each level.
  • Replay levels to improve your solution.
