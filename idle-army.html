About Idle Army: Clan Conquest

Idle Army: Clan Conquest is an idle strategy game developed by BT-Games. Grow your army, upgrade your forces and conquer new territory.

Progress even while you are away, and command your clan to victory with easy, satisfying gameplay!

Key Game Features

⚔️ Build Your Army

Recruit and upgrade your troops.

🏰 Conquer Territory

Expand your clan's power.

⏳ Idle Progress

Keep earning while you are away.

🎮 Easy to Play

Simple, satisfying mechanics.

How to Play

  • Recruit and upgrade your army.
  • Send your forces to conquer new territory.
  • Come back regularly to collect your rewards.
