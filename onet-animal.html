About Onet Animal

Onet Animal is a relaxing pair-matching puzzle game developed by BT-Games. Link matching animal tiles within 3 straight lines to clear the board.

Enjoy a cozy, classic tile-connect experience with adorable animals and levels suitable for players of all ages!

Key Game Features

🐾 Cute Animal Tiles

Charming animal artwork on every tile.

🎮 Classic Gameplay

Simple to learn, hard to master.

⚡ Helpful Boosters

Use tools when you get stuck.

📶 Offline Friendly

Play anytime, anywhere.

How to Play

  • Connect 2 identical animal tiles using up to 3 straight lines.
  • Clear all tiles from the board.
  • Use boosters strategically when you get stuck.
