About Onet Star

Onet Star (Tile Match Puzzle) is a modern, zesty pair-matching puzzle game developed by BT-Games. Inspired by classic tile-connect mechanics, Onet Star challenges players to link matching tiles within 3 straight lines to clear the board.

Whether you want a casual, relaxing puzzle session or a fast-paced time-bomb challenge, Onet Star brings vibrant graphics, diverse themes, and engaging levels suitable for players of all ages!

Key Game Features

🎮 4 Game Modes

Challenge, Endless, Limited Time, and Time Bomb modes.

🎨 Multiple Themes

Beautiful Animal, Cake & Candy, Fruit & Vegetable sets.

⚡ Useful Support Items

Shuffle, Hint, Extra Time, Bomb, and Repair tools.

📶 100% Offline Play

Enjoy playing anytime, anywhere without internet or Wi-Fi.

How to Play

  • Connect 2 identical tiles using up to 3 straight lines.
  • Clear all tiles from the board before the time runs out.
  • Use booster items strategically when you get stuck.
