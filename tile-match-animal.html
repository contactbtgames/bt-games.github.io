About Tile Match Animal

Tile Match Animal is a fun tile-matching puzzle game developed by BT-Games. Match adorable animal tiles to clear the board.

With relaxing gameplay and colorful animal graphics, it is a great way to unwind for players of all ages!

Key Game Features

🐶 Adorable Animals

Colorful animal tiles.

🧩 Puzzle Levels

Many levels to enjoy.

⚡ Helpful Boosters

Get unstuck with support items.

📶 Offline Friendly

Play anytime, anywhere.

How to Play

  • Match identical animal tiles to clear them.
  • Clear the whole board to finish a level.
  • Use boosters when you get stuck.
